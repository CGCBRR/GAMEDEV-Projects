# Super Sentai VFX Showcase

Three always-on particle effects built for the URP project, arranged side by side on a dark stage.

## Run it

1. Open `Assets/Scenes/VFX_Showcase.unity`.
2. Press **Play**.

There is nothing else to operate. All three effects loop forever and the camera is static.

## What is in the scene

| Anchor | Prefab | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| x = -11 | `Prefabs/01_HenshinTransformation.prefab` | Magical-girl / Super Sentai henshin: rotating magic circles, gold-and-pink light pillar, spiral ribbons, rising sparkles, halo rings and a petal burst. |
| x = 0 | `Prefabs/02_ElementalBending.prefab` | ATLA/LOK elemental bending: a cyan nexus orb orbited by four elemental orbs, surrounded by fire, earth, air and water displays. |
| x = +11 | `Prefabs/03_FinalBossAura.prefab` | Final boss aura: crimson/violet energy column, pulsing core, counter-rotating rune rings, lightning arcs, aura haze and ground shockwaves. |

The stage also contains a large dark floor, a dim directional fill light, a global post-processing volume (bloom, ACES tonemapping, colour adjustments, vignette, slight chromatic aberration and film grain), and a static camera at `(0, 7.2, -28)` with a 46° FOV.

## Rebuilding

Everything is generated by a reproducible editor script:

**Tools > Super Sentai VFX > Build Showcase Scene**

The builder regenerates the procedural textures, particle materials, rock mesh, prefabs and `Assets/Scenes/VFX_Showcase.unity`. Run it from Edit mode, not Play mode. Rebuilding overwrites the scene and the three prefabs.

## Asset layout

- `Textures/` – procedural PNGs (soft disc, glow, star, ring, smoke, flame, flame puff, pillar, streak, petal, magic circle).
- `Materials/` – URP particle/additive materials and the lit floor/rock materials.
- `Meshes/` – the procedural rock mesh used by the earth element and boss debris.
- `Prefabs/` – the three effect prefabs.
- `Scripts/` – small runtime motion components (`Spin`, `PulseScale`, `PulseRing`, `LightPulse`, `LightningArc`, `SpiralRibbon`) and the editor builder.

## Notes

- Effects use HDR additive sprites plus bloom, so they read as stylised anime energy rather than realistic smoke.
- All effects are self-contained prefabs; you can drop one into another scene and press Play.
- Point lights are included in the prefabs and flicker through `LightPulse`. Shadow casting is disabled for all VFX lights and particle renderers.
- The floor is intentionally glossy so the bright effects leave long specular streaks toward the camera.
